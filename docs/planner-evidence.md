# Evidence beside a habit plan

The planner now links from the action field to an evidence section in its guidance panel. The section identifies the audience, links to the selected pillar's complete practical guide and offers an optional international perspective. A native disclosure contains the existing perspective text and its cited source, publisher, type, supported claims and scope. A return link focuses the action field.

This reuses the reviewed learning-guide catalogue and its source-check date; it does not introduce new health claims, perform a new clinical review or imply institutional endorsement. The source-policy distinctions remain intact: WHO global public-health information, Harvard academic nutrition education and CDC US public-health information. NHS guidance and local urgent-help routes remain available. The Harvard and NHS frameworks are not combined into a score, and CDC adult age bands remain separate from the general NHS range.

Switching pillars closes the previous disclosure and displays the matching guide and citations. Editing, saving, restoring or importing personal records does not alter reference content. Links use static catalogue routes/URLs; no action, notes or selected Explorer settings are included. No storage access, automatic save, account, collector, schema or dependency is added.

The native disclosure and links work for the initial pillar without JavaScript. The summary has a visible focus state and touch-sized target. Browser coverage verifies all four pillar/source mappings, retained local help, keyboard jumps and disclosure reset at 320/1440px, no personal data in link URLs or outbound requests, unchanged saved data, and the no-JavaScript view.
