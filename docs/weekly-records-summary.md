# Weekly records summary

Your week at a glance is derived from the current week's seven existing records. Each day belongs to one of three mutually exclusive states: a selected choice, a non-whitespace note without a choice, or blank. The existing recorded-day count is selected-choice days plus notes-only days. Whitespace alone stays blank; adding a choice to a notes-only day does not double-count it.

The optional choice chart counts all five choice values. No choice selected includes both notes-only and blank days, so its count may be larger than the blank-day count. All bars use the same colour, with text labels and day counts available independently. No percentage score, target, streak, clinical judgement or health prediction is calculated. The scope statement explains that these are personal check-in records.

Day to open offers seven dated, labelled options. Selecting an option alone does not move context. Open selected day expands the corresponding native disclosure, focuses its choice control and scrolls it into view. Other open days are left open. The chart toggle uses a native button with aria-expanded/aria-controls. Existing seven daily disclosures remain unchanged.

The same aggregation supplies the live facts, text-download summary and plain printed summary. Original actions, notes and daily details remain in exports. The JSON backup and saved version-3 format contain only the existing week records; derived counts and navigation/display state are not stored or transmitted. The planner's measurement exclusion remains in force.

Editing a choice/note immediately recalculates the summary in page memory. Restoring, importing, changing pillars or replacing the current week reflects that current record. Viewing a summary or jumping to a day neither saves nor changes a record. Blank days may remain blank and rest/support is presented without a good/bad colour ranking.

Unit checks exercise the seven-day partition, all choices, notes-only/whitespace transitions and read-only aggregation/export of frozen records. Browser checks cover matching UI/text/print counts, real PDF generation, no automatic persistence or outbound planner/measurement requests, updates after all data-replacement flows, and 320/1440px keyboard navigation.

This is an explanation of a person's own notes, with no new medical recommendation. Future clinical-content work follows docs/editorial-sources.md and appropriate UK/global sources.
