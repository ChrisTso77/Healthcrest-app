# Content approval packet

Prepared for Chris Tsoto's actual review; **every decision below is pending**. The confirmed weekly review commitment is not a completed content review. This packet adds no health advice and does not verify professional credentials.

## Revision and review method

Review baseline: [4309697dbdc0ffb4eeacc214102f051491c4bbf8](https://github.com/ChrisTso77/Healthcrest-app/tree/4309697dbdc0ffb4eeacc214102f051491c4bbf8), the approved #122 release. Immutable source links below identify the wording being submitted; live links are navigation aids and may later change. Before signing off a later release, compare its listed content files with this baseline and review every material difference. An approval of this packet's format is not content approval or launch approval.

For each item: read the rendered text and its source record, open the cited original material, check the audience and limits, then record **Approve** or **Changes required**, the actual date, reviewer and evidence. Use the existing [source policy](../editorial-sources.md) and [scenario source-check record](../scenario-evidence-review.md); their 28 October source checks do not substitute for this review.

## Eight built-in scenarios

Open the [Habit Explorer](https://www.healthcrest.uk/engine?view=2d), choose the topic and exact example title below, then open **Guide → Scenario and evidence**. Repeat with 3D to check that the explanation does not imply a measured biological response. There is no direct scenario-selection URL: the stable slug identifies its [catalogue entry][scenarios], not a launch link. Review the entry's title, headline, outcomes, evidence label, category, sources, populations and limitations together.

| ID | Topic / exact example title | Stable catalogue slug | Specific review focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | Connected habits / Connected habits example | `integrated-harmony` | Wellbeing context does not validate combined settings, biological resilience or an overall score. |
| S2 | Connected habits / Short sleep and higher stress example | `single-pillar-failure` | Current title/wording avoids the legacy slug's implication of failure; no tissue-damage or personal-outcome claim. |
| S3 | Movement / Movement example: weekly activity | `uk-cmo-optimal` | NHS age/intensity/strength scope; legacy slug does not establish an optimal target or disease-risk reduction. |
| S4 | Sleep / Sleep example: everyday foundations | `sleep-rung1-foundations` | Routine/duration are not a sleep-quality assessment or a ranked treatment step. |
| S5 | Sleep / Sleep example: routines and support | `sleep-rung2-behavioral` | Structured CBT-I care is distinct from an illustrative setting; persistent difficulties need appropriate support. |
| S6 | Sleep / Sleep example: bedroom environment | `sleep-rung3-circadian` | General comfort/environment information, without an advanced protocol, hormone or sleep-stage claim. |
| S7 | Sleep / Research example: timed sound during sleep | `sleep-rung4-experimental` | Keep separate from everyday advice: Papalambros (13 healthy older adults) and Henin (12 nap / 19 overnight participants) have different protocols/findings; no stimulation is delivered or personal benefit predicted. |
| S8 | Stress & support / Stress example: coping and support | `active-vagal-reset` | Public coping guidance does not support a nervous-system reset, biomarker measurement or clinical-outcome claim. |

There is no built-in Food scenario in this eight-item catalogue; review Food's shared guidance and controls under C1. Unknown or mismatched CMS scenarios receive neutral **Awaiting review** wording, without accepted CMS review metadata; verify that behavior separately from approval of these eight repository entries. This packet does not approve live CMS writes or prove CMS authoring permissions.
