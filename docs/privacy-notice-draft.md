# Healthcrest privacy notice — review draft

Prepared 28 October 2026. This file is not published as a customer-facing privacy notice.

## Confirmed information

- Operator: Healthcrest Ltd, supplied by the owner.
- Public privacy/contact route: chris@healthcrest.uk.
- Email provider: GoDaddy, supplied by the owner. The exact email product, downstream processors and region still need checking.
- Website hosting/deployment: Vercel; content management: Sanity.
- No account, newsletter signup, payment flow or user-health-data upload endpoint is implemented.
- The planner keeps current edits in page memory. Only an explicit Save writes the four drafts and reflection notes to localStorage. Restore and Remove are explicit; download creates a separate user-managed text file.
- Start here puts topic/preference choices in the URL. They can appear in browser history and hosting request logs. It asks for no symptoms or diagnoses and does not upload a habit draft.
- The source contains no active analytics integration. The owner is unsure whether Vercel Web Analytics/Speed Insights is enabled. A CI lab audit cannot verify the deployed dashboard configuration.

## Draft public wording supported by current facts

Healthcrest is operated by Healthcrest Ltd. For questions about personal information, email chris@healthcrest.uk.

You can use the educational site without an account. The Start here journey uses your selected topic and practical preference to display existing guidance and a starter idea. These choices appear in the page address. They are not a clinical assessment.

Your planner notes are not sent to Healthcrest by the planner. If you choose Save on this device, they remain in this browser profile until you remove them or clear site data. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to restore them. Removing the saved draft does not delete a downloaded file.

If you email us, your email address, message and any information you include pass through our email provider, GoDaddy. Please avoid sending medical records or sensitive health information through the website contact route.

The site is hosted on Vercel and retrieves educational content from Sanity. Providers can process technical request information to deliver and protect their services. [Confirm the actual logging, retention, regions and relevant processing terms before publishing this section.]
